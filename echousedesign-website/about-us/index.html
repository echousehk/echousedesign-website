About ECHOUSE DESIGN

10+ Years of Hong Kong Architectural Excellence

ECHOUSE DESIGN is a premier Hong Kong interior architectural studio providing comprehensive interior design, space planning, and bespoke construction management.

With over a decade of proven experience across Hong Kong Island, Kowloon, and the New Territories, our team has executed over 500 high-standard interior projects ranging from luxury private estates and HOS flats to public housing transformations and corporate office headquarters.

We prioritize transparent pricing, eco-friendly material selections, strict quality assurance, and zero hidden costs—delivering peace of mind for both local homeowners and international clients.
