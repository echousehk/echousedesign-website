Wanchai Road Chinese Medicine Clinic

A 2,300 sq.ft. Chinese-inspired commercial interior on Wanchai Road. Pale timber joinery, vertical screens and illuminated shelving are set against a contemporary black ceiling and disciplined linear lighting, creating a warm and legible setting for reception, consultation and display.

Project Type

Project Budget
HKD 1,500,000
Design Direction

A calm commercial setting shaped by warm timber, vertical rhythm and measured contrast.

Fine vertical timber panelling and screen-like doors establish a familiar, ordered rhythm from the arrival sequence through to the public-facing rooms. Illuminated shelving, recessed display niches and tailored cabinetry introduce depth while allowing the material palette to remain quiet and consistent.

The project balances this warmer language with a contemporary exposed black ceiling, slim linear lighting and clear service surfaces. Long consultation tables, reception joinery and supporting rooms are resolved as connected parts of the interior, giving the commercial space a cohesive setting for display, welcome and everyday use.
