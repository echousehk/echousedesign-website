Robinson Place

A 1,115 sq.ft. modern private residence shaped for everyday family life. Pale timber joinery, full-height storage and warm display lighting create a calm foundation, while bright green, aqua and yellow colour moments bring distinct personality to the shared rooms and private zones.

Project Type
Private Residence
Design Style

Home Size

Project Budget

Design Direction

Warm timber, practical storage and colour-led rooms for a modern family home.

The shared living and dining area is organised around a long timber media wall, integrated display shelves and a low storage base. A green feature wall, yellow seating and soft lighting give the open room a welcoming identity, while the connected dining setting and kitchen support an easy daily rhythm.

Across the bedrooms, children’s room, study and bathrooms, the home continues this functional modern approach through fitted wardrobes, desk surfaces, raised sleeping elements and compact utility storage. Aqua, green and pastel joinery introduce individual character without losing the consistent warmth of the timber palette.
