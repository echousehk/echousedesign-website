One Beacon Hill

A Nordic-inspired private residence shaped around brightness, ease and practical everyday living. Across 1,570 sq.ft., light surfaces, soft blue accents, warm timber and carefully tailored storage create a calm home with a clear visual flow.

Project Type
Private Residence
Design Style
Nordic
Home Size
1,570 sq.ft.
Project Budget
HKD 1,500,000
Design Direction

Light, warmth and purposeful storage for modern family living.

The open living space is organised around a crisp white envelope, pale timber flooring and soft blue furniture and joinery accents. Integrated media cabinetry, a bright kitchen and clear circulation help the communal areas feel generous, practical and uncluttered.

Across the bedrooms, bathrooms and study corners, tailored joinery makes space for rest, work and everyday storage without losing the project’s calm Nordic character. The detailed palette of light surfaces, warm timber and restrained blue continues the visual language from room to room.
