Kornhill Garden

A 1,055 sq.ft. modern private residence in Quarry Bay, composed as a light and considered series of living zones. A calm off-white palette, dark framing and carefully placed warm light connect the rooms while integrated storage supports everyday ease.

Project Type
Private Residence
Design Style
Modern
Home Size
1,055 sq.ft.
Project Budget
HKD 1,119,500
Design Direction

Quiet contrast and streamlined living for a modern private residence.

The open living space is organised by a continuous white envelope, a low media wall and black-framed display joinery with warm internal lighting. A pale sectional sofa, tailored storage and a light-toned kitchen peninsula keep the shared areas visually open while giving each element a clear place.

Across the kitchen, bedrooms and bathrooms, the project continues its restrained material language through full-height cabinetry, carefully placed lighting and crisp marble-effect wall surfaces. The result is a composed modern home that brings practical storage, display moments and everyday rooms into one coherent visual rhythm.
