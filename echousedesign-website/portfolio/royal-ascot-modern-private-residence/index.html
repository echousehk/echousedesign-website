Royal Ascot

A 1,038 sq.ft. modern private residence in Fo Tan, arranged around pale tailored cabinetry, charcoal stone-effect surfaces and precisely integrated storage. A focused palette of warm neutrals, black-framed glass and vivid blue accent spaces brings clarity and character to the home.

Project Type
Private Residence
Design Style
Modern
Home Size
1,038 sq.ft.
Project Budget
HKD 1,558,200
Design Direction

Clean lines, layered storage and restrained contrast for a modern home.

The living and dining space is defined by a charcoal stone-effect feature wall, low pale media storage and black-framed glass display joinery. Continuous cabinetry, soft internal lighting and a window-side storage run keep the shared rooms clear and composed while offering practical places for display and daily use.

Through the kitchen, corridors, bedrooms and bathrooms, the project uses full-height cabinetry, fine black detailing and crisp stone finishes to make compact rooms work harder. A vivid blue accent appears in selected bedrooms and bathroom zones, creating individual moments within a calm, modern whole.
